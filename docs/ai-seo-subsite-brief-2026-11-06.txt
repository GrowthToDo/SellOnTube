BUSINESS AND OFFER

SellOnTube offers AI SEO services to help B2B companies acquire customers through AI assistants. AI discovery, relevant recommendations, and customer acquisition are the customer-facing premise. Website content, YouTube content, search discoverability, and optional offsite coverage are methods supporting that goal; do not present Google and YouTube as equal headline acquisition promises. Keep the audience broad initially: self-serve B2B SaaS and other B2B products. Shopify apps are one client example, not the agency’s exclusive market.

The ultimate business goal is client revenue. Relevant intermediate outcomes depend on the client: qualified leads, booked demos, installs, trials, paid activation, and paying customers. Do not present every intermediate outcome as revenue or guarantee performance.

The confirmed service scope is:
- Buying-intent blog content.
- Comparison and alternatives pages.
- YouTube content answering product, evaluation, and purchasing questions.
- Ongoing content optimization and substantive refreshes.
- Research into relevant buying questions, AI answers, competing recommendations, and content gaps.
- Measurement of AI recommendations, referrals, and acquisition outcomes where client data permits.

Package this as an AI SEO engagement, not a generic article-writing package. Explain the method after the outcome. Offsite brand mentions and relevant citation outreach are an OPTIONAL, SEPARATELY SCOPED ADDITION. Include a restrained line in the services section: “Optional: relevant offsite mentions and outreach, scoped to your opportunities.” Do not imply that outreach is included in every engagement or guarantee editorial placements, backlinks, or AI recommendations. Linked and unlinked brand mentions are distinct from backlinks. Do not silently add full technical SEO, paid placements, community management, custom analytics integrations, or other delivery commitments.

Use “AI SEO” as the primary service term, prominently in the hero eyebrow, page title, and service explanation. Explain AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) briefly in the FAQ as overlapping industry terms for improving discovery in AI answers; avoid acronym-heavy headings. Make clear that we provide a marketing service, not AI writing software.

STRATEGY CLAIMS: WHAT TO SAY AND WHAT TO AVOID

Content-led SEO is a foundation for AI search work, but publishing BOFU content does not automatically cause citations, recommendations, or revenue. Keep the public page commercially clear rather than filled with methodological caveats. Put a concise no-guarantee answer in the FAQ and attribution context in the case study.

Freshness means accurate and substantively current information, not changing dates or mass-republishing. Some observational studies find AI-cited pages newer on average than organic-search results; patterns differ by platform and do not establish that frequent refreshing causes more citations. Do not claim “AI always favors fresh content,” promise a freshness hack, or invent a universal refresh schedule.

Track meaningful trends across repeated observations rather than treating one prompt or citation as proof. Separate genuine product recommendations from simple name mentions and citations. Keep acquired customers and revenue as outcomes to measure, not substitutes for visibility metrics.
